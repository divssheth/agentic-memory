## Travel Insurance

All employees are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy (Zurich Global Corporate Travel). Coverage is automatic when traveling on approved business trips.

### Coverage Tiers

| Tier | Applies To | Medical | Evacuation | Trip Cancellation |
|------|-----------|---------|------------|-------------------|
| Standard | All domestic travel | $500K | $250K | Up to trip cost |
| Enhanced | International travel | $1M | $500K | Up to trip cost + rebooking |
| Premium | High-risk destinations | $2M | $1M | Full coverage + family notification |

### What's Covered
- Medical emergencies abroad (no deductible)
- Emergency dental
- Trip cancellation/interruption due to illness, family emergency, or natural disaster
- Lost/stolen luggage (up to $3,000)
- Flight delay expenses (meals/hotel if delay > 6 hours)
- Emergency medical evacuation
- Repatriation of remains

### What's NOT Covered
- Pre-existing conditions (unless disclosed to insurer 30 days prior)
- Extreme sports/adventure activities
- Travel against government advisory
- Personal side-trips appended to business travel (unless additional rider purchased)

## Destination Risk Categories

### Category A — No Restrictions
Standard travel approval process applies.
- United States (all states)
- Canada
- Western Europe (EU + UK, Switzerland, Norway)
- Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand

### Category B — Enhanced Precautions
Requires security briefing before travel. Register with corporate security.
- Mexico (business districts only)
- Brazil (São Paulo, Rio — business districts)
- India (major cities)
- South Africa (Johannesburg, Cape Town)
- Turkey (Istanbul, Ankara)
- Middle East (UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman)
- Eastern Europe (Poland, Czech Republic, Hungary, Romania)

### Category C — Restricted
Requires VP approval + security escort plan. Maximum 5-day trips.
- Russia
- China (specific regions — consult security team)
- Nigeria (Lagos only)
- Colombia (Bogotá only)
- Pakistan (Islamabad only)
- Egypt (Cairo only)

### Category D — Prohibited
No business travel permitted. Virtual alternatives required.
- Countries under US sanctions (North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba — check current OFAC list)
- Active conflict zones
- Countries with Level 4 State Department advisory

## Health Requirements

### Vaccinations
- Routine vaccinations must be current (company covers booster costs)
- Destination-specific vaccinations: consult corporate health portal 30+ days before travel
- COVID-19: follow destination entry requirements (no longer required for most countries as of 2026)
- Yellow fever certificate: required for select African/South American destinations

### Health Advisories
- Check WHO and CDC advisories before booking
- If a health advisory is issued AFTER booking, trip may be cancelled at no cost to employee
- Prescription medications: carry doctor's note for international travel
- Travel health kit provided for international trips (request from travel desk)

## Data Security While Traveling

- Use VPN on all public Wi-Fi networks
- Do not access sensitive systems from hotel business centers
- Enable device encryption before departure
- Report lost/stolen devices within 1 hour to IT Security
- Loaner devices available for Category B/C destinations (no personal data)
- Do not carry sensitive documents in checked luggage

## Reporting Incidents

All safety/security incidents must be reported within 24 hours:
1. Contact Corporate Security immediately for physical safety threats
2. File an incident report via the travel portal
3. Preserve any evidence (photos, documents, police reports)
4. Cooperate with any investigation
