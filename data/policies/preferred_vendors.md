# Preferred Vendors Policy

Version: 3.0
Last Updated: 2026-05-15
Effective: 2026-06-01

## Overview

Contoso has negotiated corporate rates with preferred vendors. Employees SHOULD use preferred vendors when available. Non-preferred vendor selection requires justification in the booking notes.

## Airlines

| Priority | Airline | Program | Corporate Discount | Routes |
|----------|---------|---------|-------------------|--------|
| Primary | United Airlines | MileagePlus Corporate | 12% off published fares | Domestic + International |
| Secondary | Delta Air Lines | SkyMiles Corporate | 8% off published fares | Domestic + Select International |
| Tertiary | American Airlines | AAdvantage Business | 6% off published fares | Domestic only |

### Airline Selection Rules
- Use Primary vendor when available within $100 of cheapest option
- Secondary vendor acceptable when Primary has no suitable schedule
- Tertiary vendor or non-preferred requires written justification
- Budget airlines (Spirit, Frontier, etc.) NOT approved for business travel

## Hotels

| Secondary | Marriott | Bonvoy Business | 12-20% off BAR | Global |
| Tertiary | Hyatt | World of Hyatt Corporate | 10-18% off BAR | Select cities |

### Hotel Selection Rules
- Use Primary vendor (Hilton) when available in destination city
- Secondary vendor (Marriott) acceptable when Primary unavailable or significantly more expensive (>20% difference)
- Boutique/independent hotels: Director+ level only, requires justification
- Airbnb/vacation rentals: NOT approved for business travel

## Car Rental

| Priority | Vendor | Corporate Code | Discount |
|----------|--------|---------------|----------|
| Primary | Hertz | CDP# 1234567 | 20% off |
| Secondary | Enterprise | Corp# ENT-CONTOSO | 15% off |

### Car Rental Rules
- Economy/Compact default; Midsize for groups of 3+
- SUV/Premium requires Director+ approval
- Insurance: decline rental insurance (covered by corporate policy)
- GPS/extras: not reimbursable (use phone navigation)

## Exceptions Process
- Loyalty program conflict: If employee has top-tier status (e.g., Marriott Titanium) at a non-primary vendor, they may use that vendor with a note explaining loyalty benefit
- Availability: If preferred vendor is fully booked, document unavailability
- Cost: If non-preferred is >20% cheaper, non-preferred is acceptable with justification

## Vendor Review Cycle
- Preferred vendors are renegotiated annually (Q1)
- Rate cards updated semi-annually
- Employee feedback incorporated into vendor selection
- Current agreements effective through 2026-12-31
