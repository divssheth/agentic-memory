# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Version: 2.1
Last Updated: 2026-06-01
Effective: 2026-06-15

## Reimbursable Categories

### Lodging
- Hotel accommodations at or below level-appropriate nightly rate
- Includes taxes and resort fees
- Room service for working meals only (requires justification)
- No reimbursement for minibar, spa, or personal entertainment

### Meals & Per-Diem
Per-diem rates replace individual meal receipts for trips > 1 day:

| City Tier | Daily Rate | Examples |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Tier 1 (High Cost) | $85/day | New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich |
| Tier 2 (Moderate) | $65/day | Chicago, Seattle, Berlin, Toronto, Sydney |
| Tier 3 (Standard) | $50/day | Austin, Denver, Dublin, Singapore |
| International (unlisted) | $70/day | Default for cities not listed above |

Per-diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidentals.
Company-provided meals (e.g., conference lunch) reduce per-diem by $20/meal.

### Ground Transportation
- Taxi/rideshare to/from airport: fully reimbursable
- Rental car: pre-approved only, economy class default (upgrade for Director+)
- Public transit: fully reimbursable with no receipt required under $25
- Parking: reimbursable at airport or hotel

### Airfare
- Reimbursed per booking policy (see Budget Policy by Level)
- Seat upgrades: only if within level allowance
- Baggage fees: one checked bag reimbursable; second bag requires justification
- Change fees: reimbursable if due to business need (not personal preference)

### Miscellaneous
- Wi-Fi on flights: reimbursable for flights > 3 hours
- Business phone calls: reimbursable
- Laundry/dry cleaning: reimbursable for trips > 5 days
- Tips: up to 20% on reimbursable meals/services

## Non-Reimbursable Items
- Personal entertainment (movies, sightseeing)
- Alcohol (except at approved client dinners, max 2 drinks)
- Traffic/parking violations
- Travel insurance purchased separately (company provides coverage)
- Companion travel expenses
- First-class upgrades (unless C-Suite level)

## Receipt Requirements
- Required for all expenses > $25
- Digital receipts (photo/scan) accepted via expense system
- Lost receipt: written attestation required + manager approval
- Credit card statements alone are NOT sufficient (need itemized receipt)

## Submission Deadlines
- Domestic trips: submit within 14 days of return
- International trips: submit within 21 days of return
- Late submissions (> 30 days): require VP approval
- Submissions > 60 days: denied without exception

## Currency & Exchange
- International expenses: convert at transaction-date rate
- Use corporate card for automatic conversion
- Cash advances: available for destinations with limited card acceptance (pre-approval required)
