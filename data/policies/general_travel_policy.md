# General Travel Policy

Version: 4.2
Last Updated: 2026-06-01
Effective: 2026-06-15

## Booking Requirements

### Advance Booking
- Domestic travel: book at least 7 days in advance
- International travel: book at least 14 days in advance
- Last-minute exceptions require manager approval via travel portal
- Same-day bookings: emergency only, requires VP approval

### Trip Duration
- Maximum trip duration: 14 calendar days
- Extensions beyond 14 days require Director+ approval
- Consecutive trips: minimum 3 days between trips (employee wellness policy)

### Business Justification
- All trips require written business justification
- Justification must include: purpose, expected outcome, attendees, alternatives considered
- Recurring trips (same destination monthly): one-time justification valid for 6 months

## Approval Workflow

| Employee Level | Trip Cost < Auto-Approve | Trip Cost > Auto-Approve |
|---------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Junior | Auto-approved if < $300 | Manager approval required |
| Senior | Auto-approved if < $500 | Manager approval required |
| Director | Auto-approved if < $1,000 | VP approval required |
| VP | Auto-approved if < $2,000 | C-Suite approval required |
| C-Suite | Auto-approved if < $10,000 | Board notification only |

### Approval SLA
- Manager approval: 24 business hours
- VP approval: 48 business hours
- If not approved within SLA, escalation to next level

## International Travel Requirements

- Valid passport required (6+ months validity remaining)
- Visa verification required before booking confirmation
- Travel insurance: automatically provided via corporate policy
- Register trip with Corporate Security for Category B/C destinations
- Advance booking: 14 days minimum (vs 7 for domestic)

## Cancellation & Changes

- Free cancellation: up to 24 hours before departure (most airlines/hotels)
- Change fees: reimbursable if due to business need
- No-show: employee responsible for cost unless documented emergency
- Weather/force majeure: full reimbursement via travel insurance

## Sustainability

- Prefer direct flights over connections (fewer emissions)
- Train preferred over flight for routes < 4 hours
- Carbon offset: automatically applied to all bookings (company-funded)
- Video conferencing should be considered before booking any trip
